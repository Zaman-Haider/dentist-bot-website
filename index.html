What happens behind the scenes

    From first message to booked appointment

    The same flow runs on every channel, against the same calendar and the same practice information — so a booking made by phone and one made by text message or WhatsApp can never disagree.

    1

    The patient says what they need

    A cleaning, a toothache, a whitening consult — described in plain language, on a call or in a message.

    2

    It checks who’s contacting you

    The patient’s number is recognised automatically. Returning patients are greeted with their history in view; new patients are registered in the same conversation.

    3

    It offers real, open times

    Every slot offered has been checked against your actual calendar and office hours. It never invents a time that isn’t genuinely available.

    4

    The booking is written in immediately

    Once the patient confirms, the visit goes straight into the calendar during the conversation — not into a queue for someone to type up later.

    5

    Your team stays in the loop

    New bookings, cancellations and anything needing a human are sent to the practice owner by WhatsApp and email.

    What it takes off your front desk’s plate

    Configured for your practice — your hours, your services, your doctors, your accepted insurance carriers — so every answer it gives is your practice’s answer.

    One shared calendar

    Phone, SMS and WhatsApp bookings all land in the same calendar. There is only one true schedule — never two systems to reconcile by hand.

    Insurance checked properly

    Carriers are matched against your accepted list — including the different ways patients actually say a name out loud, like “Blue Cross,” “BCBS” and “Blue Shield.”

    Returning patients recognised

    Patients who have called or messaged before are identified by their phone number — no “what’s your account number?” moment, no starting over.

    Owner notifications

    Every new booking, cancellation and item flagged for a human goes straight to the practice owner by WhatsApp and email — nothing sits unseen.

    Multilingual by default

    The receptionist replies in whatever language the patient writes or speaks in, so language is never the reason a patient gives up and calls the next practice.

    Simple commands for your team

    Staff can ask for today’s schedule, pause the receptionist on a specific conversation, or check that everything is running — with quick commands, no dashboard to learn.

    Built to never guess

    The receptionist is deliberately conservative about anything it can’t verify. It will not invent an appointment time, make up a price, or guess at a patient’s details just to keep a conversation moving. Anything outside what it can confidently handle is handed to a member of your team — and every conversation, on every channel, is kept as a full text record, so there is never ambiguity about what was said or promised.

    Insurance questions, answered honestly

    “Do you take my insurance?” is one of the most common reasons patients call — and one of the easiest to get wrong. The receptionist gives one of three answers, and only ever one it can stand behind:

    Accepted
    A carrier your practice takes

    It confirms plainly that the carrier is accepted, and carries on with booking.

    Not accepted
    A carrier your practice doesn’t take

    It says so clearly — and offers self-pay or financing options instead of leaving the patient guessing.

    Not sure
    A carrier it can’t confidently identify

    It asks the patient to repeat or spell the name. It never tells a patient “no” about a carrier it hasn’t actually recognised.

    Emergencies are urgent from the first message

    A patient in pain at midnight doesn’t get a voicemail greeting. Severe pain, swelling, a knocked-out tooth or uncontrolled bleeding is treated as urgent the moment it’s mentioned — on any channel, at any hour.

    Same-day help first

    Where an emergency slot is available, it is offered straight away — and the practice owner is notified immediately, not the next morning.

    Life-threatening signs go to emergency care

    For anything that sounds life-threatening — difficulty breathing or swallowing, serious facial trauma — the patient is told to go to the nearest emergency room or call 911, before anything else. That instruction is delivered the same way every time; it is never softened or improvised.

Everything that makes it yours lives in one place: practice name, hours, services, prices, doctors and the insurance list. Change the details and the receptionist speaks for your practice, with no change to how it works underneath.

    Done for you

    Works alongside your front desk

    A record of everything

    Questions practice owners ask

    Does it replace our front-desk team?

    No. It covers the moments your team can’t — after hours, closed days, and the calls that arrive while the desk is already busy — and it hands anything it can’t confidently handle to a staff member. Think of it as the receptionist who never sleeps, working alongside the people who never stop.

    What happens when it doesn’t know the answer?

    It says so, and passes the conversation to your team rather than improvising. It will not invent appointment times, prices or patient details. The handover, and everything said before it, is in the conversation record and the owner notification.

    Which calendar does it book into?

    One shared calendar used by every channel, so phone and message bookings can never double-book against each other. A connection to Google Calendar can be switched on if your practice wants bookings to appear there directly.

    Our patients don’t all speak English. Does it still work?

    Yes — it replies in whatever language the patient writes or speaks in, and the conversation record stays readable for your team.

    We have more than one location. Is it for us?

    The current system is built and sized for a single practice — one number, one calendar. If you run several locations, mention it on the demo call and you’ll get a straight answer about fit rather than a forced sale.

    How much does it cost?

    Pricing depends on your practice’s call and message volume, and it’s covered plainly on the 15-minute demo call — after you’ve seen the system work, so the number has something real attached to it.
